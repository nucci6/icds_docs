## **Account Expiration & Data Retention**

As a user of the ICDS Roar cluster, your access is governed by **Penn State Identity Management** policies. When your affiliation with the University ends, your Access Account will transition to a "Limited" status, which does not include HPC cluster access.

### **Access Timelines**

| User Type | Access Expiration Window | Notes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Graduating Students** | **14 Days** | The countdown starts once marked "Completed" in LionPATH. |
| **Faculty / Staff** | **14 Days** | The countdown starts on your last day of employment (Workday). |

### **Continued Access via PI Sponsorship**

If you are a graduating student or departing researcher who needs to maintain Roar access to complete research or publish papers, you may do so through a [Sponsored Account](https://security.psu.edu/services/penn-state-accts/sponsored/).

* **PI Responsibility:** Your PI or department must initiate this request via the Penn State Accounts Office.  
* **Function:** Sponsorship promotes your "Limited" account back to "Standard" status, granting you the credentials necessary to continue using Roar resources.  
* **Renewal:** Sponsored accounts must be renewed annually by the sponsoring faculty member.

### **Important Data Reminders**

* **Data Migration:** Once an account is deactivated, ICDS cannot guarantee the recovery of your data in your home and work directories. Any data in a group folder maintained by someone else may be retained according to that group’s own policies. You must move your data before your access expires unless a sponsorship is active.

* **Software Licenses:** Some software modules are restricted to active students/employees; access to these may change even with a sponsored account.

For more details, visit the [PSU Information Security Office](https://security.psu.edu/services/penn-state-accts/after-i-leave/).
